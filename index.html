NETBUILD
NetBuild is a smart, minimal personal finance app that helps you stay in control of your money without the clutter of traditional finance tools. Track transactions, write financial notes, and view your balance in real time — even offline. When you’re back online, NetBuild securely syncs your data so everything stays up to date. Clean design, intelligent insights, and total clarity for your finances.
